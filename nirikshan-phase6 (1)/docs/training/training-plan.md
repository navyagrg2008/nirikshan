# Training plan for the pilot

Goal: every pilot user can do their own role's main tasks **alone** after one session. Measure that with the UAT scripts, not with a quiz.

| Group | Who | Length | Format | Content (UAT script) | Handout |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Officers and senior officers | Verification office staff | 3 h | Desk, hands-on, one laptop each | OFF-1 to OFF-13, SNR-1 to SNR-3. Reading the score breakdown. Why rejecting needs a reason. Signing in with a code | Manual Part C |
| Inspectors | Field staff | 2 h + 1 h in the field | Own phone; then one real or mock visit with a trainer | INS-1 to INS-7. Location permission. Photos. What to do when the network is bad | Manual Part D |
| Administrators | 2 to 3 people | 3 h | Desk | ADM-1 to ADM-10, and the runbooks (backup check, banner, jobs, lost access). **Both admins must do the lost-access drill** | Manual Part E, runbooks |
| Traders and owners | A few shops and traders chosen by the office | 1 h | Group demo, then each registers one test instrument on their phone | OWN-3 to OWN-9, TRD-1 to TRD-3, check a certificate | Manual B (EN/HI), one-page QR card |
| Help desk | Whoever answers calls | 1 h | Desk | PUB-1 to PUB-8; how to read the audit trail for "who looked at my data"; when to escalate (runbook 4) | Manual A, runbook 4 |

## Materials to prepare (owner: office head)
1. Printed one-page cards (EN/HI): "Check a certificate in 10 seconds", "Report tampering", "What the system never asks for: money".
2. A test instance with **synthetic data** and one demo account per role, reset before each session.
3. A short screen-recorded demo per role (5 minutes each). Not produced in this delivery.

## Pass rule
A trainee passes when they complete their role's first 8 UAT steps without help. Anyone who does not repeats the session. Record names, dates and results in the sign-off pack.
