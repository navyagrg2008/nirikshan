# 6. Maintenance banner and feature switches

Admin > System.

## Banner
1. Type the message in **English and Hindi** (both are shown to the right audience).
2. Choose the level: information, warning or outage.
3. Tick **Show**, save. It appears on every page within a minute (the page checks every 60 seconds).
4. To remove: untick, save.
Planned downtime: switch on at least 24 hours before, with the exact start and end times.

## Switches
Four switches turn features off without a release: **public search**, **tamper reports**, **bulk register** (traders), and **self-service data requests** (owners asking for a copy, correction or erasure of their data). Use when a feature is being abused or a legal question needs time. Turning one off shows a plain "not available right now" message; it does not delete anything.

Every change is written to the audit trail with who and when.
